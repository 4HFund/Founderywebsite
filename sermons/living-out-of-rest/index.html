Living Out
of Rest.

An invitation to move beyond simply knowing the truth—and begin living in the rest Christ offers.

Pastor Josh GreenHebrews 3:1–4:13Study & reflection guide

What if rest isn't something we earn, but a way of living by faith? Follow along with Pastor Josh's study of Hebrews 3–4, reflect on the message, and capture what God is showing you.

01 / The message

Follow the study.

Tap a section to read its summary and write your notes.

01Does the Gospel Help Me Right Now?Hebrews 2:18; 3:1+

How does the gospel speak into worry, anxiety, bitterness, resentment, and everyday strain?

02We Are His HouseHebrews 3:1–6; Isaiah 66:1–2; Acts 17:24+

God’s house is his people, not a building. He intends to dwell within us.

03The Servant and the SonHebrews 3:1–6; Ephesians 3:17–19+

Moses served faithfully in God’s house; Christ is the Son over the house.

04The Little Word “If”Hebrews 3:6, 14+

Holding firmly points to a living, continuing faith—not merely outward identification.

05A Compromised PeopleNumbers 13–14; Hebrews 3+

Israel experienced deliverance yet refused to trust God when facing the giants.

06Two Warnings: Drifting and HardeningHebrews 3:7–13+

Hearing without responding can lead to drift and a hardened heart.

07The Sign of Reality: Belief vs. FaithHebrews 4:1–2+

Belief agrees with the message; faith responds and acts.

08The Goal Is RestHebrews 4:9–11+

God calls us away from self-driven strain into trust in Christ.

09The Instrument That Brings Us into RestHebrews 4:12+

God’s living word reveals old patterns and exposes what needs to change.

10Nothing Is HiddenHebrews 4:13+

We can bring our struggles honestly before the God who sees everything.

11Application: Name Your GiantsHebrews 3:12–13+

Identify one fear, bitterness, envy, or insecurity and take one concrete step of faith.

12Application: Truth Known vs. Truth DoneJames 1:22; Matthew 11:28–30+

Read a passage each morning and act on one specific truth before the day ends.

13Application: Encourage One Another DailyHebrews 3:13+

Check in with one or two people this week and pray for each other.

14Further StudyPsalm 95:7–11; Numbers 13–14; Matthew 11:28–30; James 1:22–25; Ephesians 3:16–19+

Compare these passages and reflect on the invitation to live in Christ’s rest.

03 / Go deeper

Discussion & reflection.

Use these questions personally or with a small group.

01

Which struggle—worry, anxiety, inferiority, bitterness, or resentment—most often affects you? How does the gospel speak to it?

02

How does understanding God’s house as people change your view of everyday life and community?

03

What changes when you see Christ as the Son over the house rather than a servant helping you prepare it?

04

How can you take the conditional language in Hebrews 3:6 and 3:14 seriously without living in fear?

05

What outward signs of faith might distract us from actually trusting God?

06

Where have you known a truth but resisted acting on it?

07

What is the difference between belief and faith in your own experience?

08

What does resting from your own works look like without becoming passive?

09

When has Scripture or a life experience exposed something you needed to change?

10

Who could you encourage this week, and how will you do it?

04 / Remember this

Key takeaways.

01We are God’s house; he dwells in his people.
02Christ is the Son over the house.
03Genuine faith continues and changes how we live.
04Truth acted on brings freedom.
05Belief agrees; faith acts.
06Rest means trusting Christ rather than straining alone.
07God’s word exposes old patterns and leads us toward freedom.
08Respond today and encourage one another.
05 / This week

What is your next step of faith?

Name one “giant” you are facing, choose one truth to put into practice, and encourage someone along the way.
